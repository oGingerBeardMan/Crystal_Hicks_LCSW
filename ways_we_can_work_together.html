Choosing The Right Level of Support

Every Healing Journey Looks Different

Some people benefit from the steady rhythm of weekly therapy, while others are looking for dedicated time to work through a specific concern more intensively. Neither option is "better." They simply serve different needs.

During your consultation, we'll talk about your goals, your history, and what feels most supportive for where you are today.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if I'm nervous?

Beginning therapy can feel vulnerable. We'll move at a pace that feels safe for you, and you never have to share more than you're comfortable sharing.

Do I have to talk about my trauma?

No. Therapy is collaborative. Healing can begin without discussing every detail before you're ready.

Can faith be incorporated?

Absolutely. Faith integration is always optional and entirely directed by your preferences.
